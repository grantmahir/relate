Digital Programs

Structured, self-paced programs designed to build resilience, self-awareness, and emotional regulation.

Guided growth at your own pace

Our digital programs bring the structure and depth of clinical psychology into a flexible, self-paced format. Each program is designed by a registered psychologist and delivered through the Relate Wellness platform.

Whether you're rebuilding after burnout, exploring your emotional patterns, or developing self-compassion, there's a program designed for where you are right now.

  • Psychologist-designed content and exercises
  • Work at your own pace with structured modules
  • Optional therapist support alongside the program
  • Evidence-based tools and reflective activities
