Therapeutic approaches

Grant draws from multiple evidence-based modalities, selecting and integrating approaches based on your unique presentation and goals.

  • Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) — Identifying and restructuring unhelpful thought patterns
  • Schema Therapy — Understanding deep-rooted patterns and core emotional needs
  • Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT) — Building psychological flexibility and values-based living
  • Emotion-Focused Therapy (EFT) — Deepening emotional awareness and connection
  • Solution-Focused Brief Therapy — Pragmatic, goal-oriented intervention for specific challenges

The therapy process

A structured approach to meaningful, lasting change.

1

Consultation

A brief initial conversation to understand your needs, answer questions, and determine whether therapy is the right fit. This is a no-pressure, confidential discussion.

2

Assessment

A comprehensive psychological assessment to build a clear picture of your current functioning, history, strengths, and areas for growth. This informs your treatment plan.

3

Treatment Plan

A collaborative, personalised plan that outlines your goals, the approaches we'll use, session frequency, and how we'll measure progress together.

4

Progress Measurement

Regular check-ins and validated outcome measures to track your progress, adjust the approach as needed, and ensure therapy is delivering real results.

Common questions

How long does therapy take?

The duration varies depending on your goals and presenting concerns. Some clients benefit from short-term focused work (6–12 sessions), while others engage in longer-term therapy. We'll discuss this during your consultation.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes. All services are available via secure video conferencing as well as in-person. Online sessions are equally effective for most presenting concerns.

What should I expect in a first session?

The first session focuses on understanding your background, current challenges, and goals. It's a collaborative conversation — there's no pressure to share more than you're comfortable with.
