Mental Health Tools

Interactive screening and self-assessment tools to support your wellbeing journey.

Check in with yourself

These evidence-based tools provide a quick, private way to reflect on your mental health. They're not a diagnosis — they're a starting point for understanding where you are and what kind of support might help.

All tools are anonymous and your responses are not stored.

What happens after a screening?

These tools are designed to help you reflect — not to replace professional assessment. After completing a tool, you might consider:

  • Booking a consultation to discuss your results with a psychologist
  • Exploring one of our digital programs for structured self-guided support
  • Using the insights as a conversation starter in therapy
  • Simply checking in with yourself regularly to track changes over time
