Before you reach out

Answers to common questions about getting started.

Is the initial consultation free?

The initial consultation is a brief conversation to understand your needs and determine fit. Contact us to discuss details and any associated costs.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes. All services — individual therapy, couples therapy, executive coaching, and corporate programs — are available via secure video conferencing.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

We aim to schedule initial consultations within one week of contact. Session availability varies, but we'll do our best to accommodate your schedule.
