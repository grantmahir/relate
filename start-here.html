Start Here

Your guide to getting started with Relate Wellness — step by step.

Beginning is the hardest part

We've made it as simple as possible. Whether you're seeking therapy, exploring a program, or just curious about what support looks like — here's how to get started.

Four simple steps

From first contact to your first session and beyond.

1

Book a Consultation

Reach out via our contact page or WhatsApp. We'll schedule a brief, no-pressure conversation to understand your needs and answer any questions.

Book Now
2

Complete Intake Forms

After your consultation, you'll receive a secure link to complete your intake forms. This helps us build a comprehensive picture before your first session.

3

Access the Platform

Gain access to the Relate Wellness platform where you can manage appointments, access resources, and track your progress between sessions.

4

Begin Therapy

Your first full session. Together, we'll explore your goals, establish a treatment plan, and begin the work of meaningful change.

What to expect

Starting therapy can feel uncertain. Here's what you can expect from the process:

  • Confidentiality — Everything you share is private and protected by professional ethical standards
  • No judgement — Therapy is a safe space to be honest about what you're experiencing
  • Collaboration — You set the pace. We work together on your goals
  • Flexibility — Sessions are available online or in-person, at times that work for you
  • Progress — We measure outcomes so you can see the change you're making
