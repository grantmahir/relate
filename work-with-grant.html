About Grant

Grant is a registered psychologist with a deep commitment to evidence-based practice and meaningful therapeutic outcomes. With experience spanning individual therapy, couples work, executive coaching, and organisational wellbeing, Grant brings a grounded, warm, and structured approach to every engagement.

His clinical work is informed by years of practice across diverse settings — from private practice to corporate environments — giving him a nuanced understanding of the challenges people face in both their personal and professional lives.

Therapy philosophy

Grant's approach is built on the belief that meaningful change happens within a safe, structured, and collaborative therapeutic relationship. Every person's experience is unique, and therapy should reflect that.

His work integrates multiple evidence-based modalities, drawing from cognitive-behavioural therapy, schema therapy, acceptance and commitment therapy, and emotion-focused approaches — always tailored to the individual.

  • Collaborative and client-centred approach
  • Evidence-based modalities adapted to your needs
  • Focus on measurable progress and real-world outcomes
  • Warm, structured, and non-judgemental space
  • Integration of technology to support between-session growth
