Corporate Services

Building healthier, more resilient organisations through evidence-based psychological support.

Workplace wellbeing that works

Organisations thrive when their people thrive. Relate Wellness partners with businesses to create psychologically informed workplaces — where leaders are emotionally intelligent, teams are resilient, and mental health is embedded in culture rather than treated as an afterthought.

Our corporate services are designed by a registered psychologist, grounded in clinical evidence, and tailored to your organisation's unique challenges and goals.

Our corporate offerings

Tailored solutions for organisations of all sizes.

★

Leadership Coaching

Develop emotionally intelligent leaders who drive positive organisational change. Combining performance psychology with therapeutic insight, our coaching helps leaders manage pressure, communicate effectively, and build trust within their teams.

  • 1-on-1 executive coaching
  • Leadership style assessment
  • Emotional intelligence development
  • Conflict resolution skills
  • Ongoing support and accountability
⚔

Burnout Prevention

Proactive strategies to identify and address burnout before it impacts your team. We work with organisations to build systemic protections against burnout, combining individual screening with workplace culture assessment.

  • Organisational burnout assessment
  • Individual risk screening
  • Recovery and prevention programs
  • Manager training and upskilling
  • Policy and culture recommendations
♥

Organisational Mental Health

Comprehensive mental health programs tailored to your organisation's culture and needs. From employee wellbeing workshops to mental health first aid training, we help you build a psychologically safe workplace.

  • Mental health strategy development
  • Wellbeing workshops and seminars
  • Psychological safety assessments
  • Crisis response planning
  • Ongoing consultation and support

How we work with organisations

A collaborative process designed for lasting impact.

1

Discovery

We learn about your organisation, culture, challenges, and goals through stakeholder conversations and data review.

2

Assessment

Validated tools and frameworks to establish a clear baseline and identify priority areas for intervention.

3

Intervention

Targeted programs, coaching, and training delivered in formats that work for your team — online, onsite, or hybrid.

4

Evaluation

Measuring outcomes, reporting on progress, and refining the approach to ensure sustained organisational impact.
